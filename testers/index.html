Test Lapis

Lapis gets better from real shows and real gear. If you try it, this page says what helps us most and how to tell us.

1. Get going

  1. Get a free Non-commercial key (students, teachers, charities, hobby use) or a commercial licence.
  2. Download the Bridge, start it, paste your key in the Bridge panel › Licence.
  3. Or just open Lapis in your browser: everything except talking to devices works without the Bridge.

2. What to try

  • A real show from the rider. Paste or open the rider, let Lapis (or your AI assistant) build the input list, then check what it got wrong.
  • Your paperwork. Print the input list, the stage plot and the advance sheet. Would a house crew understand them?
  • Your desk, at a rehearsal or in the shop, never during a show. Save a backup of the desk first. Connect through the Bridge, read the channel names, then send names and check every channel.
  • Your wireless. Read battery and RF from your receivers; plan frequencies for a real venue.

3. Tell us

In Lapis: Show tab › Report a problem. It saves a short text file with the version, your browser and the last errors. Your show is only included if you tick it. Send the file to info@jupiteraudio.org with a line on what you did and what you expected.

Most useful to us: the desk or receiver model and its firmware, what worked, what did not, and photos of the device screen next to Lapis.

4. Field tests on real gear

Desk drivers are tested on pretend desks so far. If you have a desk or wireless system we have not confirmed yet, write to us before you test it: we are setting up paid field tests with a short test script, and we credit every confirmed model to the person who tested it.

Keep your shows safe: Lapis saves in your browser, and browsers can clear site data. Use Save all shows (Show tab) for one backup file.
