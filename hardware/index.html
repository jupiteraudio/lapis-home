Confirmed on real hardware

Every Lapis driver follows a published protocol document, and every one is tested against a pretend device built from that document. That is not the same as a real desk. This page says honestly which devices someone has actually run Lapis with, on which firmware. Confirmed so far: 0 fully, 1 partly.

Until your device is confirmed: try it in the shop or at a rehearsal, never during a show, and save a backup of the desk first. Anything that changes a device stays off until you switch it on in the Bridge panel.

Have one of these? Run the field test (about 20 minutes) and send the result through the feedback form. Every confirmed model is listed here with your name, if you want.
