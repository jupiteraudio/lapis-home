Download the Lapis Bridge

The Bridge is a small program on your laptop. It lets Lapis talk to your desks, wireless, QLab, Smaart and the network, and it opens Lapis for you. Reading from devices is free. Sending changes to them needs a licence key: free for non-commercial use, or a commercial licence.

Install

  1. Install Node.js 18 or newer (the LTS version), once.
  2. Download the three files below into one folder.
  3. Mac: double-click Start-Lapis-Bridge.command (first time: right-click › Open). Windows: double-click Start-Lapis-Bridge.bat.
  4. Lapis opens in your browser, already paired. To send changes to devices, open the Bridge panel › Licence and paste your key.
FileWhat
lapis-bridge.cjsThe Bridge, version 2.32.0 (2.2 MB). All systems.
Start-Lapis-Bridge.commandStart it on a Mac.
Start-Lapis-Bridge.batStart it on Windows.

Check your download: SHA-256 fingerprints. The Bridge listens only on this computer (127.0.0.1), talks only to devices on private networks, and asks before it changes anything on a desk. The licence check sends only your key, once a month; never your shows or anything about your computer.
